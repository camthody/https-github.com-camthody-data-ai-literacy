About BAYK

The story behind BAYK.

BAYK started where most good things do: in a home kitchen, cooking for people I love.

What began as batch-cooking for friends who didn't have the time slowly became something more: a proper service for people who want to eat well without the mental load of planning, shopping and cooking every single night.

I cook everything from scratch, in small batches, using seasonal produce sourced locally where possible. No shortcuts, no fillers, no mystery ingredients. If I wouldn't serve it at my own table, it doesn't go in your container.

Cam

Founder and cook, BAYK
