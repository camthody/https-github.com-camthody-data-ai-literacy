# BAYK business model: one menu, three ways

## The short version

BAYK currently runs three bespoke services that all depend on Cam's time. The simpler, more scalable model is one seasonal menu each week, cooked once and sold to individuals through three packaged offers:

- **BAYK Weekly:** a subscription, and the core of the business.
- **Private dining:** a premium extra.
- **BAYK Kitchen:** a digital membership that costs almost nothing per extra member.

The Journal and newsletter bring people in.

## Where BAYK is today

- **Meal prep delivery:** pre-prepared, balanced meals delivered weekly, with calorie and dietary options.
- **Custom meal planning:** personalised plans with shopping lists and detailed recipes.
- **Private dining:** custom multi-course catering for special events, cooked on site or prepared ahead.
- **Newsletter:** a recipe email every couple of weeks.
- **Journal:** restaurant reviews and notes on ingredients and cooking.
- **Capacity:** a limited number of clients each week, with Monday delivery.

## Why it doesn't scale yet

- **Three businesses in one.** Delivery, planning and events each need their own pricing, marketing and kit.
- **Bespoke by design.** "Every plan is shaped around what you actually want to eat" means every pound earned needs Cam's time.
- **Cam is the capacity limit.** BAYK runs alongside a full-time role, so hours are the ceiling.
- **Mostly one-off revenue.** Only meal prep repeats week to week.

## The new model

One seasonal menu is set each week. Every offer is packaged from it, so planning, buying and cooking happen once.

| Offer | Customer | Format | Revenue type | Why it scales |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **BAYK Weekly** | Households and busy professionals | 5, 10 or 15 meals a week from the set menu, with dietary swaps and lighter portions | Weekly subscription | Fixed sizes, one menu, one cut-off, one delivery day |
| **Private dining** | People hosting special occasions | A few set seasonal tasting menus, limited dates | Per head, by enquiry | Premium price for capped time |
| **BAYK Kitchen** | People who would rather cook | Recipes, shopping list and prep plan from the weekly menu | Monthly membership | Almost no cost per extra member |

**What changes:**

- **Custom meal planning stops as a one-to-one service.** It becomes BAYK Kitchen, a digital membership built from work that's already done for the weekly menu.
- **Private dining moves from fully bespoke to set menus.** Clients choose from a few seasonal tasting menus instead of commissioning a new one each time.
- **No corporate offer.** BAYK focuses on individuals.

## How the pieces feed each other

1. **The Journal and newsletter bring people in.** Restaurant reviews and recipes build an audience that trusts Cam's taste.
2. **BAYK Kitchen is the low-cost first step.** People who enjoy the recipes pay a small monthly fee.
3. **BAYK Weekly is the core recurring revenue.** Members who would rather not cook upgrade to delivered meals.
4. **Private dining is the premium occasion.** Most bookings are likely to come from people who already eat BAYK at home.

## Roadmap

1. **Now: standardise.**
   - Fix one weekly menu, one order cut-off ([day and time]) and one delivery day (Monday).
   - Price the plan sizes.
   - Launch the new site.
2. **Next: prove demand.**
   - Turn the newsletter list into the first Weekly subscribers.
   - Ask every happy client for a testimonial.
3. **Then: go digital.**
   - Launch BAYK Kitchen to newsletter subscribers first.
4. **Later: take Cam off the stove.**
   - Once the menu and recipes are documented, bring in a cook or a commercial kitchen to produce the weekly menu.
   - Cam then focuses on menus, the Journal and private dining.

## Measures to watch

- Active weekly subscribers and how many stay after four weeks.
- Average revenue per order and food cost as a share of price.
- Newsletter subscribers who become Kitchen members or Weekly customers.
- Private dining bookings per month, and how many come from existing customers.
- Cam's hours per week on BAYK.
